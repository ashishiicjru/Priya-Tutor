Hackathon Project · Problem Statement: Last Mile Tutor

Great teaching, delivered to the very last learner.

A village student with no nearby coaching, patchy internet and a language barrier still deserves a patient tutor. Last Mile Tutor finds exactly what each learner is missing, builds a path from basics to pro, and teaches in their own language, even when the network drops.

The problem

Content and apps exist, but the last mile is broken: too few teachers, unreliable data, lessons in a language the learner does not think in, and nobody to notice which exact concept a student missed three classes ago. Learners fall behind silently, and gaps compound.

Our solution

🧭

Gap-finder diagnostic

A 5-question check pinpoints missing prerequisites before any plan is made.
🗺️

Basics-to-pro roadmap

Four stages: Bridge the Gaps, Core, Advanced, Pro. Weekly topics and practice tasks.
🌐

Learn in your language

Lessons, quizzes and doubts in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and more.
📶

Low-data and offline packs

Short text-only mode. Download a week of lessons once, study with no signal.
🔊

Read-aloud

Voice playback for learners who read slowly or learn by listening.
🖨️

Paper mode

Print the roadmap as a one-page sheet for classrooms with zero connectivity.
🔁

Spaced revision

Topics return for quick quizzes at the right time so learning sticks.
📲

Teacher and parent share

One tap shares a progress summary over WhatsApp or SMS.

Pitch

Problem statement

Design a tutor that reaches the “last mile” learner: low connectivity, few teachers, language barriers and unnoticed learning gaps.

How it works

Learner profile→Gap diagnostic→Personal roadmap→Lesson in own language→Quiz + revision→Teacher share
An LLM tutor generates the diagnostic, plan, lessons and quizzes. Offline packs and low-data mode shrink what must travel over the network.

Why it can win

CriteriaOur answer
InnovationGap-first planning plus offline packs and paper mode for true last-mile reach
ImpactAny subject, 8 languages, works for learners with no tutor and little data
FeasibilitySingle deployable web app, no install, runs on a basic phone browser
ScalabilitySubject-agnostic generation, so one build serves every class and skill
InclusionVernacular, voice, low-data and printable by design
